\section{Lean declaration map}
\label{app:lean}

Names are in namespace \texttt{Meanders.FirstCrossing} unless stated. The
``paper'' column identifies the statement supported by each group of formal
declarations. The correspondence is not always statement identity: the formal carrier bounds of
\cref{prop:carrier} have looser polynomial prefactors, the formal width
theorem for \cref{lem:highwidth} is the uniform bound $2(n-K)+2$ rather than
every row of its table, and \cref{thm:cost}, \cref{cor:balance} and the
resource clause of \cref{thm:main} have no formal counterpart.
