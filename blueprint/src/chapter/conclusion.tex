\section{Concluding remarks}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The threshold $K$ trades the height-bounded branch against the defect-bounded
branch; balancing the two upper bounds gives base $2$, and these estimates
yield no smaller base (for even $n$ the choices $K=n/2$ and $K=n/2+1$ give
the same sum of exponential terms). This is a statement about the bounds
proved here, not a lower bound for the construction. Two directions seem
natural: sharper key equivalences in the \HIGH\ branch (the counter component
of the key is polynomial but the pairing bound $2d+2$ is what sets the base),
and the use of the sector structure for parallel or distributed evaluation,
since sectors are independent and their layers are small compared with the
total.
